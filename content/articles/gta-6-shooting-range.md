**GTA 6's shooting ranges are their own places this time, and they're a big part of 100%.** Here's how they work, with no story spoilers.

> [!REPORTED]
> From guides by people playing the game (sources below). They agree on the two ranges and 42 golds, but quote different waiver prices.

## The two ranges

| Range | Where | Gold medals |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rodgers Ranch & Shooting Range** | Port Gellhorn (Kelly County) | 24 |
| **Griffin Shooting Range** | Balsero, Vice City | 18 |

Each range has **six courses**. Unlike GTA V, they're **not inside Ammu-Nation**.

## How it works

1. Go to the range and buy a **waiver** (a few dollars). Each range needs its own.
2. Pick a challenge and shoot for **bronze, silver or gold**.
3. Check your tally under **Progress → Activities → Shooting Ranges** in the [pause menu](article:gta-6-pause-menu-stats-progress).

## Tips for gold (reported)

- **Upgrade your guns first:** scopes, grips and bigger mags from a [gun store](article:gta-6-ammu-nation-weapons) make a real difference.
- **Don't miss at Rodgers Ranch:** players say a streak multiplier rewards accuracy there.
- **Go fast at Griffin:** speed matters more than perfect bullseyes, players say.
- Tweak **aim assist** and sensitivity in [settings](article:gta-6-best-settings) if you're struggling.

## Why bother

All **42 golds** count toward **100% completion** and a trophy. See [how long it takes to 100%](article:gta-6-how-long-to-beat).
