**Leonida is surrounded by water, and you can get a boat early.** Here's where, with no story spoilers.

> [!REPORTED]
> From guides by people playing the game (sources below). They disagree on whether any marina sells boats, so we've stuck to what most of them agree on.

## Free boats early on

- **Behind Jason's house** in **Key Lento**: a fishing boat at the dock, players report. It's also where you start [fishing](article:gta-6-fishing) and grab [scuba gear](article:gta-6-scuba-gear-diving).
- A **jet ski** sits on a **trailer** there too. Hook it to a car and back it into the water to launch it.

## More boats

- **Marinas** such as **Ocean Beach** and **Washington Beach** have smaller boats you can take.
- **Yachts and luxury speedboats** are reportedly locked until you get the **key cloner**: see [how to steal cars](article:gta-6-how-to-steal-cars).
- **Airboats** are found in **Grassrivers**, made for the swamps.

## Useful boat tips

- **Anchor:** open the vehicle menu and choose **Anchor** so the boat stays put while you fish or dive. See the [vehicle menu](article:gta-6-vehicle-menu-radio-podcasts).
- **No buying or storing:** guides say boats can't be bought or kept in a garage like cars, so leave yours somewhere you'll remember.
- Want something faster? See [stealing a fighter jet](article:gta-6-steal-fighter-jet).
