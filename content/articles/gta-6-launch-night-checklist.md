**Just picked up GTA 6? Do these 10 things and your first hours will go a lot smoother.** The first four get the game installed and ready. The rest are for once you're playing. No story spoilers.

## Before you play

1. **Free up space.** The install is reported at up to about **212 GB**. Leave extra room for updates. Download steps are in our [download guide](article:gta-6-pre-load-guide).
2. **Update your console.** Install any pending system update first, then let GTA VI finish its own update before you start.
3. **Bought the box? Redeem the code.** The physical copy has a **download code, not a disc**, and PlayStation codes are region-locked. See [physical copy](article:gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc).
4. **Pre-ordered digitally? Claim your free GTA+ month when you'll use it.** The deadline is **March 31, 2027** on PS5, or **180 days** after purchase on Xbox. See [GTA+](article:gta-6-free-month-gta-plus-redeem).

## Once you're in

5. **Change Movement Speed.** Ours was set to **Walk** by default. Turn on **Subtitles** too. See our [best settings](article:gta-6-best-settings).
6. **Learn the controls.** Square is melee, L3 runs, and the touch pad opens the map on PS5. Full list: [controls](article:gta-6-controls-ps5-xbox).
7. **Save manually.** Pause menu → **Game** → **Save Game**, with **15 slots**. Sleeping in a bed **doesn't** save. See [how to save](article:gta-6-how-to-save).
8. **Don't carry lots of cash.** You lose it if you die or get busted. More in our [beginner tips](article:gta-6-beginner-tips).
9. **Use Focus (L3 + R3)** to spot hidden things around you.
10. **Stuck loading?** Wait about 5 minutes, then fully close the game and restart your console. Other fixes: [stuck on loading screen](article:gta-6-stuck-on-loading-screen).
