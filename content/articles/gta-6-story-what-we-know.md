**GTA VI follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, a couple whose "easy score" goes wrong and drags them into a criminal conspiracy across the state of Leonida.** This page covers only what Rockstar has officially revealed before launch, so there are no story spoilers.

{{video:VQRLujxTm3c|GTA VI Trailer 2}}

## The official synopsis

Rockstar's own description, in full:

> "Vice City, USA. Jason and Lucia have always known the deck is stacked against them. But when an easy score goes wrong, they find themselves on the darkest side of the sunniest place in America, in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida — forced to rely on each other more than ever if they want to make it out alive."

## What that tells us

- **Two leads, one partnership.** Rockstar frames the story around Jason and Lucia relying on each other. Their profiles hint at the tension: for Jason, Lucia "could be the best or worst thing"; for Lucia, Jason "could be her way out."
- **A job that goes wrong starts it.** The synopsis calls it "an easy score." What it is hasn't been revealed.
- **The scope is the whole state.** The conspiracy stretches "across the state of Leonida," not just Vice City. See the [official regions of Leonida](article:gta-6-map-leonida-regions).

## Where the leads start

From the official character profiles:

- **Jason** lives in the Keys, rent-free at a property owned by smuggler **Brian Heder**, in exchange for helping with "local shakedowns."
- **Lucia** has just been released from **Leonida Penitentiary**, determined to make "only smart moves from here."

## The wider cast

Rockstar has introduced a smuggling circle in the Keys (Brian Heder and Cal Hampton), a Vice City music empire (Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest and Real Dimez of Only Raw Records), and a reckless bank robber, Raul Bautista. Full profiles: [GTA 6 characters](article:gta-6-characters).
