**GTA VI's leads are Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.** Rockstar has also officially introduced six more characters and groups: Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, the duo Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. Everything below comes from Rockstar's official profiles. We'll add in-game details after launch, clearly marked.

## Jason Duval

Jason grew up around grifters and crooks, joined the Army to shake off a troubled adolescence, and ended up in the **Keys** working for local drug runners. Rockstar's summary: "Jason wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder." Meeting Lucia, Rockstar says, "could be the best or worst thing to ever happen to him."

**In the game:** the pause menu stats screen lists Jason at **6 ft 1 in** and **217 lb** (seen on our own PS5 copy on launch day). See the [pause menu guide](article:gta-6-pause-menu-stats-progress).

## Lucia Caminos

Lucia was taught to fight by her father "as soon as she could walk." Fighting for her family landed her in the **Leonida Penitentiary** (see [the map of Leonida](article:gta-6-map-leonida-regions)); in Rockstar's words, "sheer luck got her out." Her family's history goes back to **Liberty City**, where her mother dreamed of a better life. Fresh out of prison, she's committed to her plan "no matter what it takes," and a life with Jason "could be her way out."

**Watch Lucia in the game:** the first 10 minutes of GTA VI open with her in prison. Our own PS5 gameplay, no commentary.

## Cal Hampton

Jason's friend and a fellow associate of Brian's. Cal feels safest at home, listening in on **Coast Guard** radio with a few beers. He's casually paranoid ("What if everything on the internet was true?") and content where he is, while Jason has bigger plans.

## Boobie Ike

A local **Vice City** legend who turned his time on the streets into a legitimate empire: real estate, a strip club and a recording studio. He's friendly until it's time to talk business. His biggest investment is his partnership with Dre'Quan in **Only Raw Records**.

## Dre'Quan Priest

Always more hustler than gangster, Dre'Quan dealt on the streets to get by, but music was always the goal. He has signed **Real Dimez** to Only Raw Records, and he's looking beyond booking acts into Boobie's club to the wider Vice City scene.

## Real Dimez

**Bae-Luxe and Roxy**, friends since high school, who turned "shaking down local dealers" into rap tracks and a relentless social media presence. An early hit with local rapper **DWNPLY** made them; five years and "a whole lot of trouble" later, they're signed to Only Raw Records and chasing a second hit.

## Raul Bautista

A seasoned **bank robber** with "confidence, charm, and cunning," always looking for people willing to take big risks for big rewards. Rockstar warns that his "recklessness raises the stakes with every score."

## Brian Heder

A drug runner from "the golden age of smuggling in the Keys," still moving product through his **boat yard** with his third wife, **Lori**. He lets Jason live rent-free in one of his properties, as long as Jason helps with local shakedowns.

## How they connect

Two threads tie the cast together in Rockstar's profiles:

- **The Keys smuggling circle:** Brian Heder, with Jason and Cal working for or around him.
- **The Vice City music scene:** Boobie Ike and Dre'Quan's Only Raw Records, with Real Dimez on the roster.

Raul Bautista's crew sits outside both threads in the official material. How everyone meets in the story isn't revealed yet. See [GTA 6 story: what Rockstar has revealed](article:gta-6-story-what-we-know).
