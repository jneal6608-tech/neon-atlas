## What happens in the first 10 minutes

> [!SPOILER]
>
> 1. **Prison:** the game opens with **Lucia** inside the **Leonida Penitentiary**, the prison Rockstar's own character profile says she served time in.
> 2. **The flashback:** the story jumps back to show **how Lucia ended up in prison**. You play through it, so your first moments of gameplay are part of this flashback.
> 3. **Back in prison:** the opening returns to the present. By the end of the cutscene, it looks like **Lucia is about to be released**. That matches Rockstar's profile, which describes her as fresh out of prison.
>
> After this, the story moves on to **Jason**. That's in our next video.
