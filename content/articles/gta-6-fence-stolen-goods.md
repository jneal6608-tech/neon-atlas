**Robbed a store and got a bag full of loot? You need a fence to turn it into cash.** Here's who buys what, with no story spoilers.

> [!REPORTED]
> The map listing is from our own PS5 copy. Who pays best comes from players' guides (sources below), which differ on a few details.

## Find fences on the map

![GTA 6 map Robberies tab listing Drop-offs for vehicles and goods](/assets/uploads/gta-6-map-robberies-filter.jpg)

Open the map's **Robberies** tab (money-bag icon) and look under **Drop-offs**: **Vehicles** (people who buy stolen cars) and **Goods** (fences for loot).

## Who to sell to (reported)

| Fence | Where | Best for | When |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Early fences | **Key Lento** and **Port Gellhorn** | Anything, but **low prices** | Early game |
| **Tyrone** | La Perle, Vice City | **Narcotics** | As you progress the story |
| **Yasmani** | Little Cuba, Vice City | **Jewelry** and **marked bills** | As you progress the story |

**Tip:** don't dump everything at the first fence. Prices are much better once the Vice City fences open up.

## Selling stolen cars (reported)

- **Moses** (Port Gellhorn) buys **lower-end cars without trackers**.
- **Randy** (Balsero) has a **wish list** of higher-end cars he pays well for.

How to take them: [how to steal cars](article:gta-6-how-to-steal-cars).

## Ways to hand it over (reported)

- **In person** at the fence.
- **Drop boxes** at some fences.
- **Garage pickup:** later on, you can have loot collected from your garage, for an **extra fee**. The Vintage Vice City Pack garage includes a stash spot: see the [Vintage Vice City Pack](article:gta-6-pre-order-bonus-vintage-vice-city-pack).

Where the loot comes from: [stick-ups](article:gta-6-rob-stores-stick-ups), [Smash 'n' Grabs](article:gta-6-smash-n-grab) and [gang hideouts](article:gta-6-illegal-operations-safe-codes).
