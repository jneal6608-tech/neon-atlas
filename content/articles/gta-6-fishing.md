**Fishing is one of GTA 6's most relaxing side activities, and you can start almost right away.** Here's how it works, with no story spoilers.

> [!REPORTED]
> From guides by people playing the game (sources below). We'll confirm the controls on our own copy.

## Getting a rod

- There's reportedly a **free fishing rod on the dock by Jason's house** in the Keys, so you don't need to buy one.
- **Lures** are sold at **Ammu-Nation** stores that stock fishing gear.

## Fishing controls (PS5)

| Step | Button |
| --- | --- |
| Grip the rod | Hold **L2** |
| Cast | Hold **R2**, then release |
| Attract a bite | Slowly rotate the **right stick** |
| Hook the fish | Press **R2** |
| Reel in | Steer with the **left stick**, spin the **right stick** |

On Xbox, use **LT** and **RT** in place of L2 and R2.

## Finding fish

- Use **Focus (L3 + R3)**: it highlights areas where fish are likely. Cast **just past** a highlighted spot.
- The dock by Jason's house is a good place to start.

## Why fish?

- Guides list about **35 species** to catch.
- Fishing is **catch and release**, and each catch reportedly **raises a character stat** (Focus). See your stats in the [pause menu](article:gta-6-pause-menu-stats-progress).
