**GTA 6 stuck on the loading screen? Work through these fixes in order.** They're safe and won't touch your saves.

> [!REPORTED]
> These steps are collected from troubleshooting guides (sources below) and standard console fixes. If nothing works, it's likely a server or patch problem on Rockstar's side.

## 1. Wait about five minutes

The **first load** and the first load **after an update** can take a long time. Give it about 5 minutes before you do anything.

## 2. Fully close and restart

1. Press the **PS button**, highlight GTA VI and choose **Close Game** (on Xbox: Guide button, then Quit).
2. **Restart the console** from the power menu.
3. Start the game again.

## 3. Check for an unfinished update

A **half-installed update** can stop the game loading. Check your **downloads queue** and let any update finish.

## 4. Check Rockstar's servers

If Rockstar's services are down, the game can hang while connecting. Check [Rockstar's service status page](https://support.rockstargames.com/servicestatus).

## 5. Clear the cache (PS5)

Start your PS5 in **Safe Mode** and choose **Clear Cache and Rebuild Database**, then **Clear System Software Cache**. This doesn't delete saves.

> [!WARNING]
> Don't pick **Reset PS5** or **Reinstall System Software** for this. Those are much bigger resets.

## 6. Try another save

If only **one save** won't load, try loading a different one. **Don't delete** the save that failed. See [how to save](article:gta-6-how-to-save).

## 7. Last resort: reinstall

Delete and re-download the game only after the steps above, and only once you've checked your storage space (see the [pre-load guide](article:gta-6-pre-load-guide) for the size).
