**GTA 6's legendary silenced pistol is free if you know where to look,** and players say you can grab it in Chapter 1. Here's where, with no story spoilers.

> [!REPORTED]
> From guides by people playing the game (sources below). They agree on the Permit Keys, but describe the exact spot a little differently.

## Where to find the Assassin Nipper .38

1. Get a **boat or jet ski** near **Key Lento**.
2. Head southwest toward **Maroon Key**, then keep going **west** to a narrow island in the **Permit Keys**.
3. Look for a **wrecked wooden boat** on the sand at the island's **southern end**.
4. Climb aboard and open the **small hatch in the middle of the deck**.

One guide describes the wreck as a "derelict spy vessel" at the tip of a southeastern island, so if you don't see it, check the nearby islands.

## Why grab it early

- It's a **suppressed** pistol, so shooting is less likely to bring the police right away. Handy for robberies and [staying off the wanted level](article:gta-6-wanted-level).
- It's **free**: no Ammu-Nation trip needed.

## Bonus: Smuggler Gorse 425 shotgun

Guides also point to a free **Smuggler Gorse 425** shotgun by an abandoned smuggling boat on the **small islands east of Mile Zero Air Station**.
