**Yes, GTA 6 has cheats, but they work differently from older GTA games.** Many don't work from the start: players report they **unlock as you make progress or earn achievements**.

> [!REPORTED]
> Rockstar hasn't published an official cheat list. Everything here comes from community lists and gaming sites (sources below), and they don't fully agree yet. We'll confirm codes on our own copy before listing them as fact.

## What's known so far

- **Cheats unlock over time.** Many are tied to story progress or achievements.
- **Codes use the D-pad**, the same on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **How many?** One list counts about **61** cheats, with codes for about **18** found so far.
- **Commonly listed cheats** include Unlimited Focus, Body Armor, ammo cheats (max, infinite, no reload), weather changes and screen filters.
- **The money cheat** reportedly unlocks only **after the main story**, so it won't help early on. For early cash see [how to make money](article:gta-6-how-to-make-money).

## Things to know

- **Codes on other sites may be wrong.** With a brand-new game, fake codes spread fast. Check that a list says how it was tested.
- **Cheats can bring trouble:** some may affect police attention. Keep an eye on your [wanted level](article:gta-6-wanted-level).

We'll add a full, checked code list as codes are confirmed.
