**GTA 6 has 130 stunt jumps scattered around Leonida.** Here's what players know so far, with no story spoilers.

> [!REPORTED]
> From guides by people playing the game (sources below). They agree on 130 jumps but give different totals for how many stunts 100% needs, so check your own Progress menu.

## Where they are (reported)

| Area | Roughly how many |
| --- | --- |
| Vice City and Vice-Dale | ~54 |
| Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Kelly County | ~31 |
| Keys, Grassrivers and Mariana County | ~18 |
| Mount Kalaga and the north | ~16 |
| Leonard County and Waning Sands | ~11 |

See the regions in our [map guide](article:gta-6-map-leonida-regions).

## How to land them

- Use a **fast, light car or bike**. Upgrade the engine at a [mod shop](article:gta-6-car-customization).
- Hit the ramp **straight and at full speed**.
- Guides say **just completing the jump** counts it for 100%; extra challenges on some jumps are optional.

## Tracking and 100%

- Your count lives under **Progress → Activities → Stunts**, split by region.
- Stunt jumps are one of **four stunt types** (BASE jumps are another), and 100% reportedly needs **hundreds** of stunts in total.
- More on the long haul: [how long to 100%](article:gta-6-how-long-to-beat) and [collectibles](article:gta-6-collectibles).
