**No: GTA VI is not on PC at launch, and no PC version has been announced.** Rockstar lists only **PlayStation 5** and **Xbox Series X|S** for the [November 19, 2026 release](article:gta-6-release-date-time).

> [!CONFIRMED]
> Rockstar's official GTA VI site lists the platforms as PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Rockstar has not announced a PC version or a PC release date.

## What about PS4, Xbox One, Switch or mobile?

None of them are listed. GTA VI is a **current-generation console exclusive at launch**: PS5 (including the Digital Edition and PS5 Pro) and Xbox Series X or Series S.

## Will there be a PC version later?

> [!PREDICTED]
> Our expectation, not a fact: a PC version eventually seems likely, because Rockstar brought GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 to PC after their console launches (GTA V about 19 months later, RDR2 about a year later). Rockstar has said nothing about GTA VI on PC, so **any specific date you see is a guess**.

## How to spot fake "GTA 6 PC download" claims

Because there's no PC version, anything claiming to be one is not legitimate:

- **No legitimate "GTA 6 PC download", "beta" or "early access" exists.** Files offered as one are commonly malware.
- **Ignore "leaked system requirements".** Rockstar hasn't published PC requirements because it hasn't announced a PC version.
- **Fake store listings:** the only official places to buy are the Rockstar Store, PlayStation Store, Microsoft Store and authorised retailers, and only for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

## I only have a PC. What are my options?

- **Buy a supported console.** Any PS5 or Xbox Series X|S model runs GTA VI. The Series S needs a digital copy or a code (it has no disc drive, and the physical box contains only a code anyway; see [physical copy](article:gta-6-physical-copy-no-disc)).
- **Remote Play / Xbox remote play** lets you stream from **your own** console to a PC. You still need to own the console and the game.
- **Wait for an announcement.** We'll update this page the moment Rockstar says anything about PC.
