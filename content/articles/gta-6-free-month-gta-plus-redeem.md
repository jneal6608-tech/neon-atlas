**If you pre-ordered GTA VI from the PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store before November 20, 2026, you can claim a free month of GTA+.** You claim it by joining GTA+ from the same account. Here are Rockstar's steps for each platform.

> [!CONFIRMED]
> From Rockstar's FAQ on the Rockstar Store: "If you have pre-ordered Grand Theft Auto VI from PlayStation Store or Microsoft Store before November 20, 2026, then you are eligible to redeem this offer."

## On PlayStation 5

1. Open the **PlayStation Store** and sign in to the **same account you used to pre-order** GTA VI.
2. Go to the **GTA+** page and select **Join**.
3. You'll see a confirmation that your free month of GTA+ has started.

**Deadline:** redeem on PlayStation Store by **March 31, 2027**.

## On Xbox Series X|S

1. Open the **Microsoft Store** and sign in to the **same account you used to pre-order** GTA VI.
2. Go to the **Offers & Credits** page and select **Redeem** on the GTA+ offer.

**Deadline:** redeem within **180 days** of your purchase of GTA VI.

## Things to know before you redeem

- **The month starts when you redeem, not at launch.** Rockstar's steps say the free month "has started" once you join, so you may want to wait until you'll actually use it. That's our reading of the wording; Rockstar doesn't state it outright.
- **Use the right account.** The offer is tied to the account that made the pre-order.
- **What GTA+ includes in GTA VI** hasn't been detailed on the official pages we checked. We'll add it when Rockstar publishes it.

> [!NOTE]
> GTA+ is a paid membership. When a free period ends, memberships like this usually renew at the normal price unless you cancel. Check the terms shown when you join, and cancel in your console's subscription settings if you don't want to continue.

## Didn't pre-order digitally?

Rockstar's FAQ only covers PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store pre-orders. For other retailers, check what their listing promised. The rest of the pre-order bonus is covered in our [Vintage Vice City Pack guide](article:gta-6-pre-order-bonus-vintage-vice-city-pack).
