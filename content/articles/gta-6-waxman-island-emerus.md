**There's a purple supercar with nitrous you can take for free on Waxman Island,** players report, plus a stack of cash. Here's how, with no story spoilers.

> [!REPORTED]
> From guides by people playing the game (sources below). Guides disagree on some details, noted below. The car is widely called a "McLaren" online; its in-game name is reportedly the **Progen Emerus**.

## Where it is

- **Waxman Island**, east of Vice City, at a shipping and storage yard near the island's **Pay 'n' Spray**. It's listed as a **robbery opportunity** on the map.
- The car is a purple **Progen Emerus** (some guides call it the "Purple Drank" Emerus) with **nitrous**, inside a **shipping container**.

## What else is there

- About **$10,000** in cash, players report.
- A **safe**: several guides list code **5800**, but other guides say robbery safe codes are **random**, so check yours (Focus, **L3 + R3**, can highlight notes).
- One guide also reports a **legendary pistol** in a gun case at the site.

## Keeping the car

You have to **register** the car before it's yours to keep:

- One guide says **$29,000** at any **Pay 'n' Spray**; another says **$30,000** at a nearby auto shop.
- A third guide says to take it to a **garage in Port Gellhorn** instead.

We've confirmed a **Pay 'n' Spray** has a **Register** option (see [car customization](article:gta-6-car-customization)), so that's the place to start. Check the price in your game before paying.
