**Street racing opens up partway through GTA 6 and it's a good way to earn.** Here's how it works, with no story spoilers.

> [!REPORTED]
> From guides by people playing the game (sources below). Buy-ins, rental prices and payouts differ a little between guides.

## When it unlocks

Racing **unlocks in Chapter 3** as you progress the story. A text invites you to a **race meet in Salton**, Vice City, which shows up on your map as an optional job. Do that first race, and the rest open up.

## How a race works (reported)

1. Go to the meet and talk to the organizer, then **browse races**.
2. Pay the **buy-in** (about **$300**).
3. Bring a **car** whose **rating** fits the race. Bikes don't count.
4. No car that qualifies? **Rent** one at the meet (reportedly about **$600–$900**; the priciest is fastest).
5. Win to take the prize: about **$3,000** for first.

## Finding more races

After the first one, more street races open around **Salton, La Perle, Ocean Beach** and **Gloriana Key**. Find them under **Map → Activities → Racing**.

## Win more races

- **Upgrade your car**: engine, transmission and boost at a [mod shop](article:gta-6-car-customization). The Performance tab shows your car's rating.
- **Rare cars** can be faster: see [standout vehicles](article:gta-6-standout-vehicles).
- **Watch your fuel** before a long race: see [how to get gas](article:gta-6-gas-refuel).
