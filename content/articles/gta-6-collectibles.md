**GTA 6 is packed with collectibles.** Here are the types players have found so far. They're great for exploring Leonida and count toward 100% completion. No story spoilers.

> [!REPORTED]
> From guides and interactive maps made by players (sources below). Counts and some locations still differ between guides; we'll confirm as we find them ourselves.

| Collectible | What it is | Reported count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pink flamingos** | Hidden flamingo figures around Vice City and the islands | About 125 |
| **Trading cards** | Cards hidden around the map, in sets; pawnshops sell them | About 100, in 18 sets |
| **Waink scans** | Scan landmarks and sculptures with the Waink phone app | About 112 (100 needed) |
| **Meteorite chunks** | Rare space rocks hidden in hard-to-reach spots | Not settled |

## Tips for collecting

- **Use the Waink app:** besides scanning cars ([see how](article:gta-6-waink-app-car-scanner)), you use it to scan landmarks.
- **Check the map:** the counties and regions are in our [map guide](article:gta-6-map-leonida-regions).
- **Use an interactive map** like GTABase's (linked below) to track what you've found.
- **Collectibles add a lot of time:** see [how long to beat GTA 6](article:gta-6-how-long-to-beat).
- Track your progress in the pause menu's **Progress** tab, which has a **Collections** category.
