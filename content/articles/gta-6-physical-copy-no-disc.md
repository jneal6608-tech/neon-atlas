**No: the GTA VI physical copy does not include a disc.** Rockstar says the box contains **only a download code**, which you redeem on your console to download the full game. You need an internet connection and a PlayStation or Xbox account.

> [!CONFIRMED]
> Rockstar Store: "The physical version of GTAVI will only contain a download code inside the box. A disc will not be included in the box." And: "An internet connection and platform account are required to download the game."

## What that means in practice

- **You download the whole game either way.** A code-in-box copy needs the same free space as a digital copy (reported at up to 212 GB). See our [pre-load guide](article:gta-6-pre-load-guide).
- **No disc drive needed.** Because there's no disc, the code works on consoles without a disc drive, as long as it's for the right platform (PS5 or Xbox Series X|S).
- **Pre-load is over: the game is out.** Before launch, code-in-box copies could pre-load from November 12, the day boxes started shipping. Digital copies have been able to pre-load since November 10.
- **Only the Standard Edition comes in a box.** Rockstar lists Standard as "Digital or Code in Box" and Ultimate as "Digital" only. See [editions](article:gta-6-editions-standard-vs-ultimate).

## PlayStation codes are region-locked

> [!WARNING]
> According to Rockstar, PlayStation codes bought from the Rockstar Store only work on PlayStation accounts registered in the matching region. If your account is registered in a different country from where the box ships, the code may not work.

Rockstar's regions for PlayStation codes from its store:

| Shipped to | Works with PlayStation accounts registered in |
| --- | --- |
| United States or Canada | The United States and Canada only |
| United Kingdom | The United Kingdom only |
| Any other country the Rockstar Store ships to | Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, India, Ireland, Israel, Italy, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, South Africa, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, Turkey or Ukraine |

**Xbox codes are not region-locked.** Rockstar's store says: "Xbox: Codes purchased are not region-locked."

## Shipping and cancellations (Rockstar Store)

- **Now shipping:** Rockstar estimates physical orders ship in **1–7 days**, depending on the shipping option. (Shipping started November 12, 2026.)
- The Rockstar Store ships to Austria, Belgium, Canada, Czechia, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, Ireland, Italy, Luxembourg, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland, the United Kingdom and the United States.
- **Pre-order cancellations:** the store removed its pre-order cancellation note at launch. For a problem with an order, contact Rockstar Support.

## Can you resell or lend a code-in-box copy?

A download code is usually single-use: once it's redeemed, the game is tied to that account. The store's terms mention a "single-use serial code." If resale or lending matters to you, keep that in mind before choosing a boxed copy.

> [!NOTE]
> Retailers other than the Rockstar Store may have their own bonus and shipping terms. The details above come from the Rockstar Store only.
