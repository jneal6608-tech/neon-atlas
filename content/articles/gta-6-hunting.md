**GTA 6 has hunting, and it can be a solid way to make money.** Here's how it works, with no story spoilers.

> [!REPORTED]
> From guides by people playing the game (sources below). Some older pages mixed in Red Dead Redemption 2 details, so we've only used guides written from GTA 6 itself.

## Unlocking hunting

Players report hunting **unlocks in Chapter 2** through a short **side mission** (a hunting tutorial), as you progress the story. It also opens a **hunting store** where you can **sell** what you bring in.

## How to get the best price

1. Find an animal and turn on **Focus (L3 + R3)**: its **vital spots** light up.
2. Take **one clean shot to the head or heart**.
3. That gives a **pristine** carcass, which sells for the **most**. Messy kills sell for less.

Not every animal can be sold. Check **Progress → Animals** in the [pause menu](article:gta-6-pause-menu-stats-progress) to see which ones count.

## Getting it to the store

- A **pickup truck** is easiest: you can load carcasses into the bed.
- Players say vehicles have **limited storage**, shared with other goods you carry, so check your space before a long trip.
