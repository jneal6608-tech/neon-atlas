**Yes, players have found a giant kraken under the sea in GTA 6.** It's an easter egg, not a monster that fights you. Here's what's known, with no story spoilers.

> [!REPORTED]
> From players and gaming sites (sources below). The **location reports differ**, so treat the spot as unconfirmed until we dive there ourselves.

## What it is

A massive kraken whose **tentacles are wrapped around a sunken ship**, sitting on the sea floor. Players say you can swim through parts of it, including a long hollow tunnel. Fish and rays swim around it, which makes it a great photo spot (try [Photo Studio](article:gta-6-photo-studio)).

## Where it is (reported)

- **One guide:** underwater **south of Catalan Bay** and east of Peacock Bay, off Vice City. Dive down from the second oval-shaped area off the Catalan Bay beach.
- **Another guide:** off the coast of **Vice-Dale County**, **southeast of Catalan Key**, near **Gloriana Key**.

Both put it off Leonida's east coast. See our [map guide](article:gta-6-map-leonida-regions).

## How to reach it

1. Get **scuba gear** (you'll be deep underwater).
2. Take a boat to the area and **dive to the sand**.
3. Look for the tentacles rising off the sea floor.

## Any reward?

Players report **none**: no cash, item or trophy. It's a nod to the old **GTA V kraken myth**, where fans hunted for a sea monster that never existed. This time, there's really one down there.
