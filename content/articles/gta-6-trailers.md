**Rockstar has released four official GTA VI videos: Trailer 1, Trailer 2, An Extended Look and the Launch Trailer.** Watch them below. The first three play from Rockstar's official YouTube channel; the Launch Trailer plays in Rockstar's own player on its site.

> [!NOTE]
> **Update, November 18, 2026:** the Rockstar Store's video list now shows "Grand Theft Auto VI Trailer 1", "Trailer 2", "Trailer 3" and "Grand Theft Auto VI: Pre-Load Now Trailer". Rockstar's GTA VI site still lists the Launch Trailer as its newest main trailer, and hasn't said whether "Trailer 3" is the Launch Trailer under another name. We'll add any new video here once Rockstar posts it on its site or YouTube channel.

> [!CONFIRMED]
> Every video here is Rockstar Games' own: YouTube uploads from Rockstar's channel, and the Launch Trailer from Rockstar's official site. Verified on November 15, 2026. Rockstar's GTA VI site lists the same four videos.
