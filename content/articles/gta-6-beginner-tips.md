**New to GTA 6? These 12 tips will save you money and frustration.** No story spoilers, just how the game works.

> [!REPORTED]
> Most tips come from experienced players and gaming sites (sources below); the settings and controls ones are from our own PS5 copy.

## Money and saving

1. **Don't carry a lot of cash.** Players report you lose the cash in your pocket if you **die or get busted**. Stash it at home or put it in the bank.
2. **Your bank has a limit,** one guide says, so you can't bank unlimited cash.
3. **Save by hand before big moments.** Players report there's **no mission replay**, so use a fresh slot first. See [how to save](article:gta-6-how-to-save).
4. **Early money:** see [how to make money fast](article:gta-6-how-to-make-money).

## Getting around and exploring

5. **Use Focus (L3 + R3) a lot.** It highlights enemies, useful notes (some have safe codes) and more.
6. **Dark buildings on the map can be entered,** players say; lighter ones usually can't.
7. **Follow random sounds.** Odd noises and voices can lead to hidden side content.
8. **Need a lift?** Taxis and the RydeMe app: see [fast travel](article:gta-6-fast-travel).

## Fighting and police

9. **Go stealthy early.** You start with little ammo and money, so avoiding fights is often smarter.
10. **Learn the fight controls:** weave with **X**, tackle with **sprint + Square**. See [fighting](article:gta-6-fighting-weave-tackle).
11. **Change your outfit or car to lose the cops:** police remember them. See [wanted level](article:gta-6-wanted-level).

## Settings

12. **Change two settings first:** set **Movement Speed** above Walk, and lower **Motion Blur Strength** (some players set it to 0). See our [settings guide](article:gta-6-best-settings).
