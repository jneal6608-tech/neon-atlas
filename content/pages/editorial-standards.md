These are the rules every page on {{siteName}} follows.

## 1. Sources

- **Official sources come first.** For anything about the game itself we rely on Rockstar Games (rockstargames.com, the Rockstar Store, Rockstar Support, the Rockstar Newswire) and, for console details, Sony and Microsoft.
- **Reporting from other outlets** is used only when it adds something official pages don't cover, and it is always labeled *reported* and linked.
- **Community discoveries** are credited to the person or group who found them, and published only after we've checked them ourselves or found independent confirmation.
- Every page lists its sources at the bottom with the type of each source.

## 2. Labels

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Confirmed** | Stated by Rockstar or the platform holder. We link the source. |
| **Reported** | Reported by a credible outlet, but we could not check it against an official page. |
| **Unconfirmed** | Circulating, but with no reliable source. We include these only to say they are unconfirmed. |
| **Prediction** | Our own expectation, based on stated reasoning. Never presented as fact. |

A page's overall badge reflects its weakest important claim. Individual claims inside the page are labeled where it matters.

## 3. What we don't publish

- Invented features, missions, locations or mechanics.
- Leaked material or content from unreleased builds.
- Pages written only to rank for a search term without answering it.
- Copies of other sites' work, or Rockstar's logos or text beyond short quotations with attribution.
- Official Rockstar images other than unedited press images from Rockstar's own media pages, always credited "Image: Rockstar Games" and removed on request.

## 4. Status of a page

Every guide moves through the same pipeline: *research needed → drafting → fact-checking → ready for review → published*. A published page that may be out of date is marked **needs updating** and shows a notice to readers until it has been re-checked.

## 5. Dates

Each page shows two dates: **updated** (when the page last changed) and **facts last verified** (when we last re-checked its sources). Around launch we re-verify launch-critical pages daily.

## 6. Corrections

If we get something wrong, we fix it as soon as we've confirmed the error and add a line to the page's update history explaining what changed. Send corrections through the [contact page](/contact/).

## 7. AI assistance

We use AI tools for research triage, spotting unanswered questions, and first-draft outlines. Rules:

- AI output is never published automatically.
- Every fact in AI-assisted drafts is checked against a cited source by a human editor before publication.
- AI is never used to invent gameplay details, fabricate quotes, or write fake reviews or recommendations.

## 8. Independence, ads and affiliate links

{{siteName}} is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. If we show ads or use affiliate links, they never influence what we write, and affiliate links are always disclosed (see our [affiliate disclosure](/affiliate-disclosure/)). We don't accept payment for coverage.
