{{siteName}} is an **independent, unofficial guide to Grand Theft Auto VI**. We are fans, not Rockstar Games, and we have no connection to Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.

## Why this site exists

Big game launches drown in noise: rumours repeated as fact, old leaks presented as news, and pages that never say where their information came from. We wanted a guide that does three things consistently:

1. **Answers the question first.** The answer is at the top of the page, before any background.
2. **Shows its work.** Every page lists its sources, and every important claim is labelled *confirmed* (official), *reported* (another outlet, not yet confirmed by Rockstar), *unconfirmed* or *prediction*.
3. **Stays current.** Every page shows when it was last updated and when its facts were last checked. When something changes, we update the page and note what changed.

## What we will and won't publish

Before release, we only publish what can be verified from official material: Rockstar's website, the Rockstar Store, Rockstar Support, and the platform holders. Mission walkthroughs, controls and gameplay mechanics go up **only after we have verified them in the game**. We don't write guides for things we haven't seen.

Community discoveries are welcome and credited to the people who found them, once we've been able to check them.

## How we use AI

We use AI tools to help organise research, spot questions people are asking, and suggest outlines. Every published page is written or reviewed by a human editor and checked against its sources before it goes live. We never auto-publish AI-generated text. Details are in our [editorial standards](/editorial-standards/).

## Artwork and official images

Our illustrations, logo and design are our own; we don't copy Rockstar's logos, fonts or website design. Official character images come from Rockstar's public media pages, are credited "Image: Rockstar Games" wherever they appear, and are used only to illustrate our coverage. Trailers play from Rockstar's official YouTube channel. Rights holders can ask us to remove any image through the [contact page](/contact/), and we will.
