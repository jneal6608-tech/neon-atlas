# 30-day editorial strategy (Nov 15 – Dec 14, 2026)

Goal: become the most trustworthy quick-answer source for launch-month GTA VI questions, and get indexed with a coherent set of 40–60 genuinely useful pages, not hundreds of thin ones.

Targets below are **output targets we control**, not traffic forecasts. A new site may take weeks to be indexed and ranked, so the first month is about building the right pages and learning from Search Console.

## Week 0: Nov 15–18 (pre-launch)

- Deploy and verify Search Console; submit the sitemap; request indexing for the top pages.
- Re-verify launch-critical pages daily (the report flags them).
- Publish from official sources: **Soundtrack & radio stations** (bl015) and **Collector's box** (bl016), after verifying both on Rockstar's pages.
- Nov 18: the launch-night checklist auto-publishes.

## Week 1: Nov 19–25 (launch week, highest priority)

Publish in this order, each only after in-game verification:

1. How to switch between Jason and Lucia (keyword priority 95)
2. Controls for PS5 and Xbox (83)
3. Stuck on loading screen / crashing fixes (79)
4. How to save (79)
5. Best settings (graphics modes, camera, aim)
6. Beginner tips (15 verified tips)
7. Does GTA 6 have GTA Online? (publish the fact-checked status on day 1)

Also: confirm or correct the "reported" claims (size, unlock time, Ultimate price) and upgrade their labels.
Target: 7–10 new pages; every launch-critical page re-verified daily.

## Week 2: Nov 26 – Dec 2

- Mission list hub plus the first walkthroughs (template from bl024), written as you play.
- Money guide, wanted level, safehouses and properties.
- Vehicles: expand the named-vehicles page into a full database (where to find, our own tests).
- Map: expand each region with points of interest.
- Start weekly Search Console imports; act on CTR and striking-distance findings.

Target: 10–14 new pages, plus updates to 5+ existing pages.

## Week 3: Dec 3–9

- Collectibles hub, cheats (if they exist), hunting and fishing (officially referenced for Mount Kalaga), fastest cars (tested).
- "How long is GTA 6" from your own playthrough time.
- Characters: add in-game details, spoiler-tagged.

Target: 8–12 new pages.

## Week 4: Dec 10–14

- Story completion pages: ending explained (spoiler-tagged), endings and choices if they exist.
- Consolidate: merge thin pages, add internal links (`node tools/editorial.mjs links`), refresh titles with low CTR.
- Review month-one data: which sections earn impressions; plan month two around them.
- Monetisation readiness review (see MONETIZATION.md). Apply for AdSense only if the checklist is met.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Mon | Search Console import and report; plan the week from data |
| Tue–Fri | Play, verify, publish (2–3 pages/day in launch weeks, 1–2 after) |
| Sat | Update pass: re-verify, add internal links, fix flagged pages |
| Sun | Rest, or catch up on the backlog |

## What success looks like after 30 days

- 40–60 pages, every one answer-first, sourced and verified.
- 0 known factual errors left uncorrected; update history on every changed page.
- Search Console shows impressions across launch-week and first-month queries. Those queries then decide month two.
